GlobalBuy Privacy Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use GlobalBuy. We understand the critical importance of your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data with the utmost care and transparency.

By using our service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

1. Definitions

Key Terms

Account: A unique, personalized account created for you to access specific features within our service.
Application: GlobalBuy, the comprehensive software program developed using the FlutterFlow platform.
Company: GlobalBuy, with registered office at Huttenstraße 25, 13353 Berlin, Germany. We act as the Data Controller under GDPR.
Personal Data: Any information that directly or indirectly relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
Service: The GlobalBuy application and all its functionalities.
Usage Data: Data collected automatically from your use of the service.

2. Data We Collect

Personal Data

We may collect the following personally identifiable information:

  • Email address - For account registration, login, and notifications
  • Name - For personalization and shipping purposes
  • Phone number - For order verification and delivery notifications
  • Address details - For order processing and shipping
  • City, State, ZIP/Postal code - For delivery and shipping calculations

Usage Data

Automatically collected information includes:

  • IP address and browser information
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Device identifiers and mobile device information
  • Diagnostic data for app performance
  • Location data (with your explicit permission)

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

While GlobalBuy doesn't extensively use tracking cookies for advertising, the FlutterFlow platform and integrated services may use cookies to:

  • Maintain user sessions
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze usage patterns
  • Facilitate secure transactions

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

Essential Services

  • Service provision and maintenance - Operating GlobalBuy effectively
  • Account management - Creating and maintaining your user profile
  • Contract performance - Processing orders, payments, and deliveries
  • Customer support - Responding to inquiries and resolving issues

Communications

  • Transactional emails - Order confirmations, shipping updates, security alerts
  • Marketing communications - Special offers and product updates (with your consent)
  • Service notifications - Important updates about our service

Business Operations

  • Analytics and improvement - Understanding usage patterns and enhancing our service
  • Security and fraud prevention - Protecting your account and our platform
  • Legal compliance - Meeting regulatory requirements

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK, we process your data based on:

  • Contract performance - Necessary to provide our service and fulfill orders
  • Consent - For marketing communications and optional features
  • Legitimate interests - For service improvement, security, and analytics
  • Legal obligation - To comply with applicable laws and regulations

5. Data Sharing

We may share your information in the following situations:

Service Providers

  • Payment processing - Fapshi payment gateway (Cameroon-based)
  • Cloud hosting and analytics - Firebase and other FlutterFlow integrated services
  • Customer support - Third-party support platforms

Business Operations

  • Corporate transactions - Mergers, acquisitions, or asset transfers
  • Affiliates - Other companies within our corporate group
  • Business partners - For co-branded offers or integrated services

Legal Requirements

  • Law enforcement - When required by valid legal requests
  • Legal compliance - To meet regulatory obligations
  • Safety protection - To protect users and prevent illegal activities

Public Areas

Information you share in public areas (reviews, comments) may be visible to other users and distributed outside our service.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for:

  • Service provision - While your account is active
  • Legal requirements - As mandated by applicable laws
  • Legitimate interests - Fraud prevention, dispute resolution

Usage data is typically retained for shorter periods and may be aggregated or anonymized.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred outside your jurisdiction. For transfers outside the EEA/UK, we ensure adequate protection through:

  • Adequacy decisions - Countries with recognized data protection standards
  • Standard contractual clauses - Legal contracts ensuring data protection
  • Your explicit consent - When appropriate safeguards are explained

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you're in the EEA or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access - Request copies of your personal data
  • Rectification - Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Erasure - Delete your data under certain conditions
  • Restrict processing - Limit how we use your data
  • Data portability - Transfer your data to another service
  • Object - Oppose processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent - For consent-based processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@globalbuy24.com or through your account settings.

9. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures including:

  • Encryption - Protecting data in transit and at rest
  • Access controls - Limiting data access to authorized personnel
  • Regular audits - Assessing and improving security practices
  • Secure storage - Using reputable, secure data centers
  • Staff training - Ensuring employees understand data protection

While we use industry-standard security measures, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and protect your account information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

For users aged 13-16 in jurisdictions requiring parental consent, we may require parental approval before processing their data.

11. Third-Party Links

Our service may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. For significant changes, we will:

  • Post the updated policy with a new "Last Updated" date
  • Notify you via email or in-app notification
  • Give you time to review changes before they take effect

Your continued use of the service indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
